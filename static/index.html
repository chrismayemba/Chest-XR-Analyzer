Chest X-ray Caption Drafting

BLIP + LoRA adapter, fine-tuned on a filtered chest/thorax subset of ROCOv2-radiology.
Not a diagnostic tool. This model drafts a free-text caption from an image. It has not been clinically validated, its training data is figure legends from published articles (not radiology reports), and its best validation loss (5.90) indicates an early-stage checkpoint. Every caption below is an unreviewed AI draft — treat it as a starting point for expert review, never as a finding or a diagnosis.
